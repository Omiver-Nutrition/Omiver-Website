# Surface brief: omiver-website public marketing site

Scope: `/` (core/index.html), `/information`, shared header/footer, contact block; `/terms` and `/privacy` inherit the system. Mode: **Persuade**.

Audience & job: health- and performance-conscious individuals deciding to create an account and order the at-home kit. Primary action: **Create Account** → `/app/register`. Secondary: contact form, `/demo`.

Constraints: keep all existing copy verbatim (typo fixes only); keep sage brand green and OMIVER wordmark; use real assets (`omiver_box.png`, `report_screenshot.png`, `three_device_screenshot.png`, `platform.svg`). Non-diagnostic disclaimer stays in footer. No invented claims, prices, testimonials.

Chosen direction: the category standard (canon), executed at full craft. Quality bar: Function Health, InsideTracker, Levels, Oura. Critique reference: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/canon.png` (build path: code-led per tool; session flipped to comp, so the build matches this comp's composition closely).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The convention a buyer of at-home health testing already trusts, done better than its neighbours; refuses the incumbent's dated pill buttons, mauve fills, and boxed-everything layout.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white green-tinted canvas (#f7f9f8 / white), ink #0f1720, sage #67997d for tints and data, deep sage #436e57 for every filled action. One clean sans (Figtree) at a confident scale, generous whitespace, 20px-radius soft panels on pale sage, real product photography with rounded corners, thin hairlines.

STORY: Visitor sees the kit and the promise, understands at-home blood → 100+ molecules → regimens that adapt, believes it through the real report and platform screenshots, then creates an account or contacts the team.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar (wordmark left; How Omiver Works, Demo, Login, filled Create Account right). Left ~50%: H1 at ~64px, subtitle, Create Account (filled) + Contact us to Learn More (outline). Right ~45%: the real kit-box photo, rounded 24px. Bottom edge: three benefit panels begin.

FORM: Canon (category standard), list position n/a (standing exit chosen over a bolder hand). seed:9d833fd9693d0141
